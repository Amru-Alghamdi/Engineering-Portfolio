Equations used

Assumptions and limits

  • Normal conditions 0 C and 1.01325 bar with Z = 1 (22.414 Nm3/kmol). Standard (15 C) conditions are not used.
  • Pressure is absolute; Z must come from an EOS or chart for the actual gas.
