Equations used

Q_i [kW] = n_i [kmol/h] * 1000 * (-dH_i) [kJ/mol] / 3600Q_total [MW] = (Q_CO + Q_CO2) / 1000Steam [t/h] = Q_total [kW] * 3600 / dh_steam / 1000

Assumptions and limits

  • dH varies with temperature: standard values at 25 C are about -90.6 and -49.5 kJ/mol; at 250 C they are roughly -97.9 and -58.1 kJ/mol. Edit to suit.
  • All released heat is assumed recovered by the steam; heat losses and sensible heat of gas ignored.
  • Steam enthalpy gain = steam enthalpy minus boiler feed water enthalpy at drum pressure.
