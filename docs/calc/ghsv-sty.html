GHSV and Space-Time Yield

Gas hourly space velocity and catalyst space-time yield from flow, catalyst volume and production.

Inputs
Nm3/h
m3
t/h

Results

Equations used

GHSV [1/h] = Q_gas [Nm3/h] / V_cat [m3]STY [t/(m3 h)] = m_MeOH [t/h] / V_cat [m3]

Assumptions and limits

  • Gas flow at normal conditions (0 C, 1.01325 bar).
  • Settled bulk catalyst volume; STY in t/(m3 h) equals kg/(L h).
  • Useful for comparing against vendor ranges and tracking catalyst ageing; not a kinetic model.
