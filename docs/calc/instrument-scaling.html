Equations used

PV = LRV + (mA - 4)/16 * (URV - LRV)mA = 4 + 16 * (PV - LRV)/(URV - LRV)Q = Qmax * sqrt((mA - 4)/16)mA = 4 + 16 * (Q/Qmax)^2

Assumptions and limits

  • Signals outside 3.8-20.5 mA (NAMUR NE43) are flagged as out of range or possible fault.
  • Low-flow cutoff: square-root extraction amplifies noise near zero DP (1% DP is already 10% flow), so flows below the cutoff are normally forced to zero in the DCS.
  • Assumes the transmitter is ranged in DP and square root is applied once (in transmitter or DCS, not both).
