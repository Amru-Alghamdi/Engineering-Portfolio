Equations used

Q = m * Cp * dT (kW, m in kg/s)Counter: dT1 = Th,in - Tc,out ; dT2 = Th,out - Tc,in. Co-current: dT1 = Th,in - Tc,in ; dT2 = Th,out - Tc,outLMTD = (dT1 - dT2) / ln(dT1/dT2) (arithmetic mean when dT1 ~ dT2)R = (Th,in - Th,out)/(Tc,out - Tc,in) ; P = (Tc,out - Tc,in)/(Th,in - Tc,in)F = [sqrt(R^2+1)/(R-1)] ln[(1-P)/(1-PR)] / ln{[2-P(R+1-sqrt(R^2+1))]/[2-P(R+1+sqrt(R^2+1))]}R = 1: F = [P*sqrt2/(1-P)] / ln{[2-P(2-sqrt2)]/[2-P(2+sqrt2)]}A = Q / (U * LMTD * F)Air: m_air = Q/(Cp_air * dT_air) ; rho = P*M/(R*T), M = 28.964 kg/kmol ; V = m_air/rho ; A_face = V/v_face ; W_fan = V*dP/etaApproach = T_process,out - T_ambient

Assumptions and limits

  • Design duty uses the mean of hot and cold duties; a warning is shown if they differ by more than 2%.
  • Bowman F is for one shell pass and an even number of tube passes; F below 0.75 is a sign to add shells.
  • Constant Cp and U; no phase change; fouling must be included in U.
  • Air density evaluated at fan inlet (ambient, forced draft). Fan power is air power divided by efficiency (no motor/drive losses).
  • Approach badge: 15 K or more OK, 10-15 K marginal, below 10 K poor.
