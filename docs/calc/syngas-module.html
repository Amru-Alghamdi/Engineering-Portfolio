Equations used

M = (yH2 - yCO2) / (yCO + yCO2)y_blend,i = (F_mu*y_mu,i + F_rc*y_rc,i) / (F_mu + F_rc)Gap = M - M_target

Assumptions and limits

  • Compositions are normalised to 100 mol% if they do not sum to 100 (a warning is shown).
  • Flows must be on the same molar basis (kmol/h or Nm3/h).
  • Typical makeup target for methanol synthesis is about 2.0-2.1; the recycle/blend module is normally much higher because H2 accumulates.
  • Ignores methanol, water and minor species in the recycle.
